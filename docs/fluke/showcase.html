A story with an animated piece in it

Scroll down. The piece below is plain HTML from Fluke — no embed, no iframe — so it arrives with the page.

What to look for: the yellow panel wipes away, the kicker drops in, the headline springs up, the rule draws itself, the logo and ring zoom in, the stats arrive one after the other with a bounce, the diamond wobbles and the dot keeps pulsing. Scroll past it and back and it plays again. Narrow the window (or open it on a phone) for the phone layout. With “reduce motion” switched on, it simply shows.

Story text carries on above and below the piece, as it would in Shorthand or the Story Engine. Everything in the piece is real text: the headline is an H2 in the page outline, the stats can be selected and read by screen readers, and the logo has alt text.
